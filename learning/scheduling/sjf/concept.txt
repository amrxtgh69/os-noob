SJF (Shortest Job First) exposes the Oracle Problem.

Key insight: SJF requires knowing future job runtimes, which is impossible in practice.

Example showing why it's unimplementable:
- Process A: arrival=0, runtime=100 (but we don't know this yet)
- Process B: arrival=0, runtime=1 (but we don't know this yet)
- Process C: arrival=0, runtime=50 (but we don't know this yet)

Without knowing runtimes, we cannot choose the shortest job. Any prediction mechanism will be imperfect.

Learning focus: Understand that optimal scheduling requires future knowledge, pushing us toward algorithms that learn from past behavior (like MLFQ).
