Round Robin (RR) exposes the Trade-off Between Response Time and Turnaround Time.

Key insight: RR provides good response time by giving each process frequent CPU bursts, but at the cost of increased turnaround time due to context switching overhead.

Example showing the trade-off:
- Quantum too small: excessive context switching, poor throughput
- Quantum too large: behaves like FCFS, poor response time for short processes

Learning focus: Understand that RR solves the response time problem of FCFS but introduces a new optimization challenge - choosing the right time quantum. This motivates MLFQ's approach of using multiple queues with different quantums to balance both metrics.
