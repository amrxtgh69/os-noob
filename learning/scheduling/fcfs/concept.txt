FCFS (First-Come, First-Served) exposes the Convoy Effect problem.

Key insight: Short processes get stuck behind long processes, leading to poor average turnaround time.

Example scenario that shows the problem:
- Process A: arrival=0, runtime=10
- Process B: arrival=1, runtime=1  
- Process C: arrival=2, runtime=1

Execution: AAAAAAAAAA B C
This demonstrates how short processes (B,C) suffer due to arriving after a long process (A).

Learning focus: Understand why FCFS is unsuitable for interactive systems due to poor response time for short jobs.
